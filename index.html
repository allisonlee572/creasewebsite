Fold letters into paper airplanes and fly through papercraft worlds in this story-driven game about the struggles between a daughter and distant father.

Explore a young girl's shifting understanding of family as she grows up with her father working abroad. As her letters, fold carefully into unique paper planes. Deliver yourself safely and swiftly. Navigate three levels filled with origami life as their relationship unfolds around you.
